How to ask for help

One main channel, so nothing gets lost in side conversations.

The channel

Send an email to [SUPPORT-EMAIL].

You immediately get an automatic confirmation with a reference. That reference follows the case to the end — always reply in the same thread.

On Standard and Complete you also get direct WhatsApp with your named contact, for anything urgent.

What to include

With this, we almost always fix it first time:

  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead
  • Who is affected — one person or the whole team
  • The address of the page where it happens
  • A screenshot, if you can

If it is not resolved

A short, explicit ladder. You never have to work out who to complain to.

  1. The support threadReply to the same email. The case keeps its reference and full history.
  2. Deadline missed → escalateIf your plan’s first-response time is missed, reply in the thread with the word ESCALATE. The case is raised a priority level immediately and the service lead is notified.
  3. Review with the service leadWe book a call within 1 business day to agree a resolution plan with a date.
  4. Service creditIf monthly availability falls below the commitment for two consecutive months, you are entitled to a credit on the next invoice, under clause 6 of the Terms.

Availability and maintenance

We commit to 99.5% availability in each calendar month. That is the number we can honestly hold to — we would rather promise what we sustain than write a bigger figure we cannot.

  • Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 48 hours ahead and done outside working hours whenever possible.
  • Daily backups, retained under the retention policy, on infrastructure in the European Union.
  • Your data leaves whenever you want — exporting training, hours, certificates and evidence is available at any time, in an open format, without depending on our software.
An honest note on incidents. When something goes wrong we say what happened, what we did, and what we changed so it does not repeat. No vague statements.

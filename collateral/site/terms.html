11Training framework — what the Service is and is not

This clause exists to avoid any misunderstanding about the legal reach of what the platform produces.

  • The Service produces supporting documentation for the employer's compliance with continuous-training obligations, namely the record of hours and the corresponding evidence. It is not legal advice and does not replace the Client's own legal assessment.
  • Certificates issued are internal training certificates, carrying the Client's brand. They are not professional certification issued by a certified training body (DGERT), nor do they confer a formal qualification or registration in SIGO.
  • The evidence record proves the integrity and non-backdating of records — that is, that a given record existed on a given date and was not altered afterwards. It does not prove, and does not purport to prove, the pedagogical quality of the training or the substantive truth of what was taught.
  • Verifying the Client's compliance with its legal obligations before the competent authorities, namely the ACT, is the Client's responsibility.
